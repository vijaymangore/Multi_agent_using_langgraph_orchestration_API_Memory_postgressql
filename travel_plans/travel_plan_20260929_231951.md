- **Afternoon/Evening**: Board Manila‑Goa flight. Overnight on the plane (or in a Manila hotel if you prefer a long layover).

## 5️⃣ Practical Tips

| Topic | Advice |
|-------|--------|
| **India e‑Visa** | Apply 4‑7 days before travel (tourist, 30‑day stay). Philippine passport holders are eligible. |
| **Currency** | Indian Rupee (INR). ATMs widely available in Goa; keep some cash for beach shacks. |
| **Transport** | Rent a scooter (≈ USD 5‑7 / day) for short hops; use pre‑booked taxis or a private car for longer transfers (Cebu‑Manila‑Goa → hotel). |
| **Health** | Carry basic meds (motion sickness, stomach upset). Drink bottled water only. |
| **Dress code** | Light cotton, modest attire for churches (cover shoulders/knees). Flip‑flops for beach, sandals for town. |
| **Connectivity** | Buy a local SIM (Airtel/ Jio) at the airport – ~USD 10 for 15 GB data (30 days). |
| **Safety** | Goa is generally safe; keep valuables in hotel safe, avoid isolated beaches after dark. |
| **Peak season** | November‑February = best weather, higher prices. October (your travel month) is shoulder season – good balance of crowd‑size and cost. |
